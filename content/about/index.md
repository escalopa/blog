Hey, I'm Ahmed Helaly — a backend engineer focused on **Go, distributed
systems, and reliable high-load services**.

I build and operate production microservices, design service architecture, and
work across product and platform boundaries. My recent work includes services
handling up to **250K requests per second**, reducing p99 latency from **50 ms to
5 ms**, integrating government and banking APIs, and owning the architecture and
delivery of a critical courier-service initiative.

Alongside product engineering, I have taught Software Architecture at Innopolis
University and built open projects including a Raft key-value store, a Go
microservice framework, and a serverless prayer-times bot. This site is where I
turn those interests into practical engineering notes and tutorials.

I was born in **Alexandria, Egypt**, and work in an international environment in
English, Arabic, and Russian. I earned a bachelor's degree in Software
Engineering from **Kazan Federal University** in 2024 and am completing a
master's degree in Software Engineering at **Innopolis University**.
